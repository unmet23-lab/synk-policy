01 / FILM

From plan to final cut, made for where it runs

We start with where it will be used and who will see it. You get files sized for your website, product pages and social channels, and we choose AI video, live shooting or both, whichever suits the brand.

01

Product ads

A good fit for — brands whose product strengths are clear but do not come across in photos and long copy

Short ads that show the details and real use of the product, plus cuts for each channel from the same footage.

For example

“Tumbler · show how easy it is to carry · for social ads”

  1. We receive product photos and colour references
  2. We film the carrying scenes and details as an ad
  3. You get vertical cuts and ad images too
What we make
A product ad, social cuts, and images for product pages and ads
What changes
Customers decide after seeing the features in use, and you do not have to commission each channel separately.
02

Brand films and commercials

A good fit for — companies whose introduction changes depending on who gives it

One film that shows what the company does and what it does well, with cuts for the website, sales and events.

For example

“Company deck and brand story · one message for an event”

  1. Narrow it to one message
  2. Make the brand film around it
  3. Deliver website and social cuts too
What we make
A brand film with cuts for website, sales and events
What changes
Everyone shows new customers the same introduction.
03

AI models and lookbooks

A good fit for — brands that constantly need on-model shots and usage scenes for product pages and social media

Product images, lookbooks and fashion videos with the AI model you choose. Colour and fit are checked against reference photos.

For example

“Clothing photos · colour and fit references · chosen model and mood”

  1. Make on-model images from the references and chosen model
  2. Check colour and fit against the references
  3. Extend into lookbooks and fashion video
What we make
Product images, lookbooks and fashion videos with an AI model
What changes
One model and mood keep the whole range consistent, and new products continue with the same model.
04

Service and how-to videos

A good fit for — companies spending time explaining how to use their service and answering the same questions

Introductions and how-to videos made from the real screens in the real order, with subtitles and channel cuts.

For example

“Real service screens · the order of use · common customer questions”

  1. Order the video by the most common questions
  2. Show the steps on the real screens
  3. Check the narration is clear, then finish
What we make
Service and how-to videos with subtitles and channel cuts
What changes
Customers follow along with the video, and staff stop repeating the same explanation.

Many videos, one brand

We use two Atlas engines in production: Loom for the picture and Reed for sound. The product, people and style rules set at the start carry into every later video.

  • Set the rules first — Reference photos of the product and people, what must be shown, and what we will not do.
  • Finish for each channel — Loom lays out scenes and motion; we edit for social, website and event screens.
  • Listen before delivery — With Reed’s checks we play back levels, transitions and narration before we finish.

A project starts by pinning down six things

  1. The brand and the purpose of the video
  2. The audience and the one message
  3. Mood, model and scenes to include
  4. Channels and whether it runs as a paid ad
  5. Photos, footage, logos and reference shots you have
  6. Length and schedule

This is made-to-order production: you choose the deliverables. Length, formats, revisions, usage rights, quote and schedule are agreed per project.
