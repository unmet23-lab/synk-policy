Video production

From plan to final cut, made for where it runs

We start with where it will be used and who will see it. We show you every cut as a scene image before the video, and deliver files sized for your website, product pages and social channels.

  1. Product ads

    Short ads that show the details and real use of the product, plus cuts for each channel from the same footage.

    Who it is for
    Brands whose product strengths are clear but do not come across in photos and long copy
    What you get
    A product ad, social cuts, and images for product pages and ads

  2. Brand films and commercials

    One film that shows what the company does and what it does well, with cuts for the website, sales and events.

    Who it is for
    Companies whose introduction changes depending on who gives it
    What you get
    A brand film with cuts for website, sales and events

  3. AI models and lookbooks

    Product images, lookbooks and fashion videos with the AI model you choose. Colour and fit are checked against reference photos.

    Who it is for
    Brands that constantly need on-model shots and usage scenes for product pages and social media
    What you get
    Product images, lookbooks and fashion videos with an AI model

  4. Service and how-to videos

    Introductions and how-to videos made from the real screens in the real order, with subtitles and channel cuts.

    Who it is for
    Companies spending time explaining how to use their service and answering the same questions
    What you get
    Service and how-to videos with subtitles and channel cuts

How we make it

Three checks before the video: the direction and model, the one line the ad should leave and its written storyboard, and a scene image for every cut.

  1. Every cut firstNot one or two key frames but every cut in the ad, shown at final quality before the video. A scene image is the first frame of its shot, so small details can shift once it moves; products, logos and text are composited and do not change.
  2. A cast you choosePick from twenty-one AI models with fixed names and faces. The next ad continues with the same model and the same caption style.
  3. Real products, logos and on-screen textWe never redraw them; we composite your photos and files, so text does not blur and logos do not change.
  4. Decide what it leaves firstThe first line of the plan decides whether the ad stays with people through a story or through one striking image.
  5. Sound firstWe make the voice and music first and cut to their beat. Shots that fall short are made again, not covered up in grading.
