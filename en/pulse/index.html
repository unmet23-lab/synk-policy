How we make it

Three checks first: direction and model, the ad’s takeaway and storyboard, and a still image per shot.

  1. Every shot firstNot one or two key frames but every shot in the ad, shown at final quality before the video. A still image is the first frame of its shot, so small details can shift once it moves; products, logos and text are composited and do not change.
  2. A cast you choosePick from twenty-one AI models with fixed names and faces. The next ad keeps the same model and caption style.
  3. Real products, logos and on-screen textWe never redraw them; we composite your photos and files, so text does not blur and logos do not change.
  4. Takeaway firstThe plan’s first line decides how the ad stays with people: a story, or one striking image.
  5. Sound firstWe make the voice and music first and cut to their beat. Shots that fall short are made again, not covered up in grading.
