02 / 03

Using & licensing our work

Listening or viewing and commercial use are different. We review the purpose, media, and duration with you.

How it works

What to clarify before use

  1. Where will the work be used?
  2. In which media, and for how long?
  3. What scope of use do you need?
More details ＋

We review the intended use, media, and duration for music, video, or characters and agree on the scope. Public availability for listening or viewing does not mean permission for commercial use.

03 / 03

Co-production

We plan work around a shared goal, then agree on production responsibilities, rights, and how it will be released.

How it works

What we will decide together

  1. The message the work should convey
  2. Each party's production responsibilities and rights
  3. Where and how to release the work
More details ＋

We plan a work together around the purpose of the collaboration. We agree on the production scope, rights, and release plan, then develop it in a way that suits the work.
