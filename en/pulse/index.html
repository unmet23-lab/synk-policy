Ad & model video production

From ads to model films,
made to fit your brand

We make product ads, brand films, AI model images and lookbooks, and how-to videos, from planning to the finished piece. You receive them in formats ready for your website, product pages, and social media.

Together we choose between AI video and live shooting, whichever suits the brand.

01

Product ads

Brands whose product has clear strengths that photos and long text fail to convey

We turn the product’s details and real use into a short ad, and make edits for each channel from the same source.

Example

“A tumbler · want to show how easy it is to carry · for social media ads”

  1. We receive product photos and colour references
  2. We make an ad with carrying scenes and details
  3. You also get a vertical edit and ad images
What you get
A product ad, edits for social media, and images for product pages and ads.
What changes
Customers see the features and the product in use, and decide whether to buy. You do not need to commission each channel separately.
02

Brand films & commercials

Companies whose introduction changes with whoever gives it

We put what your company does and does well into one film, with edits for your website, sales, and events.

Example

“A company brochure and brand story · one message to deliver at an event”

  1. We settle on a single message
  2. We make the brand film around it
  3. You also get edits for your website and social media
What you get
A brand film and edits for your website, sales, and events.
What changes
Anyone can show a new customer the same introduction. The same film works in proposals, on your website, and at events.
03

AI models & lookbooks

Brands that keep needing worn shots and in-use scenes for product pages and social media

We make product images, lookbooks, and fashion films with the AI model the brand chooses. Colour and fit are checked against reference photos.

Example

“Garment photos · reference photos for colour and fit · the chosen model and mood”

  1. We make worn images from the references and the chosen model
  2. We check colour and fit against the references
  3. We extend them into a lookbook and fashion film
What you get
Product images worn or used by an AI model, a lookbook, and a fashion film.
What changes
One model and mood keep the whole range consistent. When a new product arrives, you can continue with the same model.
04

Service explainers & how-to videos

Companies that spend time explaining how to use something and keep getting the same questions

We make service introductions and how-to videos with the real screens in the real order, with subtitles and edits for each channel.

Example

“Real service screens · the order of use · questions customers often ask”

  1. We order the video around the frequent questions
  2. We show the process on the real screens
  3. We check the narration is easy to hear, then finish
What you get
A service introduction or how-to video, subtitles, and edits for each channel.
What changes
Customers follow along with the video by themselves. Your staff do not have to repeat the same explanation.

This is custom production: you choose the results you need. Length, format, number of revisions, usage scope, quote, and schedule are set for each project.

What Atlas does

However many pieces we make,
they look like one brand

Atlas is the name for the eight engines SYNK builds itself. PULSE uses two of them in production: Loom, which composes the screen, and Reed, which handles sound. The product, people, and style standards set at the start carry over to the next video.

01

We set the standards first

We gather reference photos of the product and people, what must be shown, and what to avoid. The next scenes use the same standards.

02

We finish for where it will be used

We compose each scene’s layout and motion with Loom, and edit for social media, your website, and event screens, so there is less to rework after delivery.

03

We check the sound too

Using Reed’s standards, we play back the loudness, the joins between scenes, and the narration before finishing.

We also use other tools suited to each project when generating and editing AI images and video.

Client brief

Your video,
organized in six steps

A brief to fill in before you commission a film. As you choose and write, who you are speaking to, what you say and how you show it come together on one page.

  1. 01

    Brand & purpose

    Note the name, the purpose of the video, and the customers you want to reach.

  2. 02

    Customers & strengths

    Sum up what customers want to know, why they would choose you, and the message that must come across.

  3. 03

    Mood & scenes

    Choose the mood, models, and scenes, and mark which scenes must be included.

  4. 04

    Usage plan

    Note the channels, whether ads are paid, the prices or offers to announce, and what you want viewers to do after seeing the ad, such as enquire or buy.

  5. 05

    Materials

    Select the photos, videos, and logos you have, and pick reference photos for colour, detail, fit, and length.

  6. 06

    Review & send

    Check the key summary and file list, then send the downloaded file.

What you write is kept only on your device for now; send us the downloaded file yourself. Quotes and schedules are agreed separately in consultation.
