AI models, real people and your rights

How we label the AI models in your ad, what we will not make, where you can use the finished ad, and what happens to anything you send us.

Updated 17 November 2026

Every person on screen is an AI model

Every person and voice in our films is made with AI. Our twenty-one models were made with AI. Yuho is based on SYNK founder Yuho Yang, with the founder’s own consent; the other twenty are virtual, not real people.

How the AI label appears on your ad

Under Korean rules in force since 1 June 2026, any ad in which an AI-generated virtual person recommends or endorses a product must disclose that the person is virtual.

In a video, the label sits near the person while they are on screen. In text, it goes in the title or the opening. Before the ad goes out, we check the label’s wording and position with you.

If your category has its own disclosure and ad-approval rules, we include the wording and its placement in the first draft, and confirm the final wording with you.

If you run the ad as a paid ad, we also take care of the disclosures and specs required for ad review.

For the final call on how your ad must be labelled, please check with the platform you advertise on and your legal adviser.

Real people

We don’t take requests to make anyone look like a specific real person. Reference photos show the feel you’re after. We won’t make a persona look like anyone in them.

The one exception is a model based on a person who agrees to it themselves, such as your CEO or a member of staff. We aren’t taking requests to feature a real person yet.

When we do, we will make such a model only after we confirm that person’s consent. We will confirm it separately with that person; choosing an option in the brief won’t count as their consent. The model can be kept for future ads only if that person has agreed to its reuse.

Where you can use the ad

You can use the finished ad, including its music and voices, commercially across online ads, social media, your own online store and product pages. For TV, outdoor, cinema or print, ask us first.

Please make sure you have the rights to the photos, logos, music and copy you send us. The brief asks you to confirm this.

Once you publish the finished ad, we may show it as SYNK PULSE work. If you’d rather we didn’t, tell your producer and we’ll leave that ad out.

Industry exclusivity — whether other brands in your industry can use the same model — is agreed when we discuss your project. The contract sets out ownership and usage rights, delivery files, revisions and support.

What you send us, and when it is deleted

We use your brief and contact details to review your request and make your ad.

Photos you send for a custom persona, and the persona we make for you, are used only for that brief and are kept with its records. If the brief doesn’t lead to production and there’s no activity for 90 days, we delete those photos and the persona. If the brief goes into production, they stay with its records and have no set deletion date.

To use a custom persona in future ads, your brand admin must separately consent to us keeping it for 12 months. Thirty days after that 12-month period ends, we delete the persona’s images and files, including the photos you sent. If you consent again before then, you can keep using it. If an ad with this persona is still in production on that day, we delete the images and files once that ad is finished. Ads already delivered to you are not deleted.

After deletion, copies remain in our server backups for up to 13 months before they’re erased. They are never added to our separate long-term archive.

If you’d like your photos or a persona deleted, tell your producer. Deletion can’t be undone.

The rest of your brief — your answers, contact details and the product files you send — stays with your project records. We have not yet set a retention period for these.
