LAB’s educational philosophy

Learn Korean
Learn to think for yourself

We prepare students for studying in Korea and the life that follows. They work towards TOPIK Level 4, and we teach them to make their own judgements even when they use AI.

What all seven games share

  1. 01
    Act on what you hear and read

    What you understand decides what happens in the game: run where the request says, make the drink that was ordered, stamp by the notice.

  2. 02
    Look back after each round

    After each round you see what you got right and what you missed. Words in Rhythm reviews with new audio questions, and The Teacher Is Back gives new text to read.

  3. 03
    One learning record

    All seven games share this device’s learning record, and Atlas uses it to choose what comes next. The record stays in this browser.

Listening and reading in the games, speaking and writing in class. TOPIK I tests listening and reading; writing is in TOPIK II and speaking in TOPIK Speaking. The games are foundational practice and do not guarantee a pass. TOPIK / NIIED ↗

Why games and a narrator ＋

Learning principles supported by research, built into play.

Game-based second-language learning

Dixon et al. (2022) reported a positive average effect on second-language learning in between-group comparisons (d=0.50). This informed our decision to connect play with language choices.

Dixon et al., 2022 · Language Learning & Technology ↗
Answer actively and receive feedback

Yang et al. (2021) synthesized 222 studies with 48,478 learners and found an average achievement benefit from quizzing (g=0.499). This supports active answering, checking answers, and revisiting questions.

Yang et al., 2021 · Psychological Bulletin ↗
Combine visual and spoken information

Modality-effect research reports benefits from combining spoken and visual information, with outcomes varying by complexity and pace. We therefore combine short cues, optional captions, replay, and time to choose.

Reinwein, 2012 · Journal of Psycholinguistic Research ↗

These are average effect sizes from external research, not percentages or measured SYNK results. Retention, understanding of new questions, and TOPIK score changes in beginner Korean learners require separate evaluation.

All seven games are playable prototypes. Ten minigames are in preparation, with conversations with village residents as the next step. There is no sign-in, and the learning record stays in this browser. Racing’s 400 learning stages share the same sunset coastal course.
