THE REASON

Why I startedSYNK.

I studied architecture and psychology, took part in performance and creative projects, and helped run an educational institution in Mongolia. These were different fields, but each involved understanding people and creating something together. Those experiences now inform my work on SYNK.

People may study the same language for very different reasons: to attend university, work in Korea, or start a business. Their goals and the support they need will differ. I want to build a company that combines learning with practical experience to help people prepare for what they want to do.

AI has opened up more ways to turn ideas into something tangible. I’m learning where technology can help by building things and improving them myself. At SYNK, I’m developing a personalization system that considers a person’s goals and current circumstances to suggest relevant learning and activities. As their goals change, I want the support to change with them.

WHAT MATTERS

What matters to meas I build SYNK.

01

Start with why someone wants to learn.

When preparing for Korean study or TOPIK, I also think about what the learner wants to do afterward. Attending university classes and talking with colleagues call for different kinds of practice and experience.

02

Make room to try things yourself.

I want lessons and activities to give people a chance to express their thoughts, hear different views, and complete a shared task. By working through what didn’t go well, I hope they can gradually do more for themselves.

03

Technology frees up time for what matters.

I want technology to help with repetitive tasks such as organizing information and copying it from one place to another, leaving more time for conversation and creating new things. I also value the enjoyment and good memories that can come from learning and working together.
