THE REASON

Useful technology startswith understanding people.

AI lets us find answers faster and create more. That makes it even more important to take time to think about why we learn, what we want to do, and whom we want to help. I want technology to give people more room and more opportunities to find their own answers.

Studying architecture and psychology, performing on stage, and working in education in Mongolia have each given me a different perspective: an eye for structure, an interest in how people think and act, and experience creating something with others. At SYNK, I bring these perspectives together to design support around each person's goals and circumstances.

People learning the same Korean language may have very different goals. One wants to attend university; another wants to start a business. Those goals can change through experience. I want to build a system that looks at what a person needs to learn now and what they could do next, without defining them by an initial score or a single choice. That is the technology I hope to create through SYNK.

Yuho Yang · SYNK

WHAT MATTERS

More possibilities,through your own experience.

01

Start with the goal.

Korean language skills and TOPIK are important preparation for opportunities to study and live in Korea. We think together about what to learn and where to use it, so that this preparation connects to each person's goals.

02

Experience should become your story.

I value the experience of expressing your thoughts, listening to different views, and following through on your role. You should be able to explain not just the result, but what you tried and changed along the way, in your own words.

03

Technology can give people more time.

I want to spend less time repeatedly organizing and copying information, and more time learning, talking, and creating. Enjoyment and good memories along the way matter too.
