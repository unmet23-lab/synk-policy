Story

I want to raise students who can become leaders

As deputy director of Skyline Edu, an academy in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, I was in charge of student counselling and running classes. What mattered most to me then was knowing each student: what they were good at, where they got stuck, what they valued and how they studied. Only then could I help them in the way they needed. But I did not have the time to do that for every student. So I began building a personalization system, and that is where SYNK’s technology began.

The near-term goal is for students to reach TOPIK Level 4. Studying in Korea usually takes Level 3, and without Level 4, graduation and jobs come with many restrictions. More students than you might think come to Korea hoping to work. So I try not to make learning that is fun but leaves no real skill behind.

Today anyone can make a lot of things quickly with AI. But working with AI, it is easy to be pulled along by whatever it produces. To tell an AI “this is right, this is wrong, try again”, you need to think critically. And for that, you first need to know what you like and dislike, and what kind of life you want.

That ability grows by doing. You need to make something from your own ideas, put it out there, take criticism and get feedback you did not expect. Learning Korean has many places for that kind of experience. My ultimate goal is to help students who learn this way become leaders.

Yuho Yang · SYNK

Values

Three things I hold to while building SYNK

  1. 01

    The goal comes first

    Two people learning the same Korean may want different things: one wants a Korean university, another wants to start their own business. So what to learn first and which TOPIK level to aim for follow each person’s goal. Goals change along the way, so a plan is never fixed by a single first score.

  2. 02

    Learn by doing

    I think it matters to say what you think, hear other views and see a role through to the end — and to explain in your own words not just the result but what you tried and why you changed it. What I care about most is that learning is enjoyable and that the feeling of having done it carries into the next challenge.

  3. 03

    Time saved by technology goes to learning and experience

    Repetitive work like organizing and copying material goes to technology. The time it frees up should go entirely to people feeling, experiencing and learning for themselves. Good memories along the way matter too.
