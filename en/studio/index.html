What’s ready · PULSE app

From brief to delivery,
in one app

Below are actual screens from the app.

  1. BriefNine screens and it is done. Pass any field you are unsure of with “We’ll suggest”, and if you stop midway you can pick up where you left off. The price and your delivery date show right there.
  2. Choosing direction and modelYou receive two directions together with models, and choose. Outfit and hair are also chosen from photos at this point.
  3. Written storyboard and scene imagesYou see every shot first in words, then in pictures. If we need anything more, we ask only about the missing fields.
  4. Video review and deliveryYou receive files in the aspect ratio that fits where you will post. Products and logos are not drawn with AI; we use the real photos you send.
