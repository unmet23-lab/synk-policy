Installing and opening the Mac preview

1. Open Apple menu → About This Mac. Choose Apple Silicon for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor. Both versions require macOS 13 or later.

2. Open the downloaded DMG, drag SYNKPlatformPreview into Applications, then launch it from Applications. Use the same steps when installing WORLD from the platform.

3. If macOS cannot verify the developer, confirm that you downloaded the app from SYNK, then use Open Anyway for that app in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Each app may need this confirmation the first time it opens.

Atlas

Atlas is eight engines

Eight engines share the work: understanding a person’s goal and situation, fitting the content, screens, words and sound to them, and checking whether it actually helped. The more someone uses it, the better the next suggestion fits.

How we work

01

We do not guess from age

We go by the settings a person chooses and what they actually did, not age, gender or nationality.

02

Your answers and AI guesses stay separate

What you wrote and did is kept as the original; anything AI inferred is marked so you can correct it.

03

If it is not ready, we say so

We mark what you can use today separately from what we are still building, and we do not promise effects we have not checked.

I started SYNK because I wanted to give every student the attention they needed.

Yuho YangFounder, SYNK

As deputy director of an academy in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, I knew each student needed different help, but I never had the time to tailor it to every one of them. Atlas is my attempt to do that with technology.
