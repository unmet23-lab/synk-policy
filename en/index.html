Korean & study

Foreigners who want to live in Korea
pass through these three.
The threshold is TOPIK Level 4.

We open in February 2027. We are preparing now.

  1. Korean language

    And Level 4 is not easy

    36%

    Share of TOPIK II test-takers in Mongolia who reached Level 3 or above (2025, National Institute for International Education). Level 4 is a higher bar.

  2. Study in Korea

    Without Level 4, working hours are capped

    max 10hrs a week

    Hours a third- or fourth-year undergraduate may work without Level 4 (Ministry of Justice rules). With Level 4 it is 25 hours a week.

  3. Settling and work

    Few places keep helping after you arrive in Korea

    43.3%

    Language: the top difficulty named by foreigners living in Korea (2022 survey, analysed by Statistics Research Institute). Loneliness came second.
