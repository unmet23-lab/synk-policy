SYNK ATLAS

Atlas, the personalization engine SYNK builds

Atlas is the personalization engine SYNK builds, and the name for its eight engines together. They share the work, from deciding what a person needs to ordering what to learn, giving explanations and hints, shaping screens and sound, and checking whether the help worked. LAB, SHIFT, PULSE, and PATH each pick the engines they need and fit them to their own services.

SYNK builds
a personalization engine

SYNK builds a personalization engine. We started with Korean education. In classrooms in Ulaanbaatar, we saw that every student had different strengths, got stuck in different places, and had different reasons for learning, so we set out to use technology to teach each student in a way that fits them. The engine we built is Atlas.

LAB handles Korean education and personalized learning; SHIFT introduces and operates personalized services for companies; PULSE creates ads, model videos, content and IP; and PATH prepares personalized travel and support with study, careers and settling in Korea. We are first connecting the engines with Korean learning games and apps as we develop them. Our aim is to bring the same engines to games, study, work and consultation, for people from their twenties to their sixties.

We build all eight engines ourselves

Eight engines share the work, from deciding what is needed to ordering what to learn, recording what you tried, giving explanations and hints, and checking whether the help worked. The same team makes the felt screens, the music, and the sound, so what we tailor to one person can also reach them through what they see and hear.

We don’t guess from age

We adapt to the settings you choose and the results of what you actually tried, not to your age, gender, or nationality. When your goal changes, the next practice and suggestion change with it, and we tell you why we prepared them.

We check whether the help worked

Finishing something is not proof that you improved. We look separately at whether what you did without help got better, whether the suggestions were right, and whether it was comfortable to use. We do not roll out a new method if results get worse for any group, whether people in their 20s or their 60s.

We keep your answers apart from AI’s guesses

Your own answers, what you actually did, and what a teacher observed are kept as originals. Anything AI corrected or inferred is marked separately, so you can check it and correct it.

Built for anyone, built to last

You choose larger text, shorter explanations, and ways to use it without sound, and we refine each step so a first-time user knows what to do next. Free features calculate on your device without going through an AI server, so the service can keep running as more people use it.

We build the engine, services, and content to these standards. We tell you what is still in development and what you can use now; see each brand page for what is available.

PATHFree public experience

Seoul, your way

Save places in Jongno and compare a half-day plan against your time and budget.

See the problem and process
The problem
A list of attractions alone does not help you judge your date, budget and time at each stop.
The process
We connected places with official visitor information to interests and visit records. Compare a date, arrival time and budget, then build a route with your own visit durations and travel allowances.
The result today
Choose places, compare a half-day plan and displayed costs, identify information that needs checking and take away an itinerary note.
Before you use it
This is not a booking service. Travel allowances are entered by you, not live walking routes. Recheck opening details and prices before visiting. Consented records stay in this browser.

LABPublic prototype

Words in Rhythm

Play along to music, listen to a Korean sentence and question, then answer with O or X.

See the problem and process
The problem
When rhythm skill and Korean comprehension share one score, it is hard to tell what needs practice.
The process
We connected play and answers through two buttons, quieted the music and cleared normal notes during questions, and recorded timing and listening answers separately.
The result today
Play three songs, review listening answers and practise with another sentence. It targets information in short utterances and meaning agreement, skills relevant to TOPIK listening.
Before you use it
This is a practice prototype, not an official test or level assessment. TOPIK score gains and learning effects have not been validated with students. Use it with sound on.

LABPrototype gameplay previews

Listen, read and answer with your hands

Watch real scenes from Korean games: making orders, running along a path and responding in a conversation.

See the problem and process
The problem
A description alone does not show what a learner actually does or practises in a game.
The process
We connected listening and reading inputs to actions and rewards, captured short clips of real play, and described the controls and practice in each game.
The result today
Open previews of eight games on LAB and compare the different practice activities.
Before you use it
These are real clips of prototypes. They do not imply that every game has formally launched or that learning effects have been established. The introduction explains each game's practice and development scope.

A consistent face for a brand

Explore a model's appearance and expression before planning ads, lookbooks and brand content.

See the problem and process
The problem
When a model's appearance and expression change between pieces, a brand has to explain its visual direction again.
The process
We first assembled reference images and expression directions, then connected each model to possible image and video uses on the PULSE page.
The result today

These are in-house images, not client delivery or campaign-performance cases. Yuho is based on the founder; the other five models are fictional. Work based on a real person requires that person's consent.
