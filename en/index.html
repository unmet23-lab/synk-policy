04 / HOW WE WORK

How we build

01

We do not guess from age

We go by the settings a person chooses and what they actually did, not age, gender or nationality. When the goal changes, the next suggestion changes too.

02

We check whether it helped

Finishing something is not proof of progress. We look separately at whether people can now do it without help and whether our suggestion was right.

03

Your answers and AI guesses stay separate

What you wrote and did is kept as the original. Anything AI inferred is marked so you can check and correct it.

04

We make the screens and sound ourselves

One team makes the felt screens and characters, the music and the voices. So we can fit not only the content but how it looks and sounds.

05

Built to be used for a long time

People choose large text, shorter explanations or a sound-free mode. Free features compute on the device, so they keep running even as more people use them.

06

If it is not ready, we say so

We mark what you can use today separately from what we are still building, and we do not promise effects we have not measured.
