A scene preview. This page does not judge your speech; in the pilot, once the scene ends, the coach listens and points out just one thing. The characters and the university are fictional.

  1. 01Characters react, not scores

    If your line doesn’t land, the other character asks again, in character. No grades.

  2. 02One note at a time

    After the scene: endings, politeness or a missing word — one note, and only when it is sure.

  3. 03Measured on unseen lines

    Only what you say on your own in a new situation counts — not lines you memorized.

SYNK WORLDWhat changes as you use itLive · Preview

Wrong answers return in a minute. Right ones, just as you’d forget.

Try one question →Why it works this way →

When you open it

What should I do today?

You don’t have to choose. When your record changes, so does what your tutor says.

A minute later

Wait, what was that phrase?

A phrase you miss returns a minute later, in a new scene. Not the same question again: the same expression in a different situation.

From tomorrow

When do I see it again?

It comes back without you tracking dates. A phrase you got right returns tomorrow. Keep getting it right and the gap widens to 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days.

Anytime

Am I getting better?

See the evidence, not a score. Each skill keeps a line for when, where and whether you got it on your own.

On a day off

I don’t feel like studying

Then play a game. Game answers go into the same record, and what you miss there, your tutor brings back as your next practice.

1, 7 and 30 days on

Memorized, or actually known?

One right answer isn’t the end. The same skill comes back as a question you haven’t seen, so you see for yourself whether you still know it a month later.

Principles we keep

  • We do not guess from ageWe go by the settings a person chooses and what they actually did, not age, gender or nationality.
  • Your answers and AI guesses stay separateWhat you wrote and did is kept as the original; anything AI inferred is marked so you can correct it.
  • If it is not ready, we say soWe mark what you can use today separately from what we are still building, and we do not promise effects we have not checked.

Installing and opening the Mac preview

1. Open Apple menu → About This Mac. Choose Apple Silicon for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor. Both versions require macOS 13 or later.

2. Open the downloaded DMG, drag SYNKPlatformPreview into Applications, then launch it from Applications. Use the same steps when installing WORLD from the platform.

3. If macOS cannot verify the developer, confirm that you downloaded the app from SYNK, then use Open Anyway for that app in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Each app may need this confirmation the first time it opens.
