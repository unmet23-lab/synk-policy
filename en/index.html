How we built these

PATH · Free

A day in Seoul

The problem
A list of places does not tell you whether they fit your date, budget and how long you want to stay.
What we built
We connected real places in Jongno, Bukchon and Seochon to your time, budget, walking and companions. You see why each place was suggested and can keep, swap or drop it.
What you can do now
You get a day of 2–5 places with a map and walking times. If you run late or change your mind, only the rest of the day is replanned, and you can keep the trip in your SYNK ID.
Before you start
It covers the Jongno area of Seoul only. Check walking times, opening hours and prices before you go. There is no live navigation, booking or payment.

LAB · Public prototype

Words in Rhythm

The problem
If rhythm-game skill and Korean listening share one score, you cannot tell what to practise.
What we built
Two buttons handle both playing and answering, and the music and notes drop back while a sentence or question plays. Timing and listening answers are recorded separately.
What you can do now
Play three songs, listen to short Korean sentences and answer O or X. It practises finding information and checking details, as in TOPIK listening.
Before you start
It is not an official test or a level result. We have not yet checked with students whether scores improve. Please turn the sound on.

LAB · Free

Korean that starts with play

The problem
Explaining what you will learn does not show what you actually do in a game or what it practises.
What we built
If you cannot read Hangul yet, you start with play: look, listen and copy. Once it feels familiar, you move on to games where you act on what you hear or read.
What you can do now
Start right away, from Word Magic, Your First Shop and Fix the Spaceship to listening and reading games. In WORLD, supported game progress and decorations stay with your account.
Before you start
Game results are not TOPIK levels or pass predictions, and learning effects have not been checked yet. Progress without signing in is kept separately from your WORLD account.

04 / HOW WE WORK

How we build

01

We do not guess from age

We go by the settings a person chooses and what they actually did, not age, gender or nationality. When the goal changes, the next suggestion changes too.

02

We check whether it helped

Finishing something is not proof of progress. We look separately at whether people can now do it without help and whether our suggestion was right.

03

Your answers and AI guesses stay separate

What you wrote and did is kept as the original. Anything AI inferred is marked so you can check and correct it.

04

We make the screens and sound ourselves

One team makes the felt screens and characters, the music and the voices. So we can fit not only the content but how it looks and sounds.

05

Built to be used for a long time

People choose large text, shorter explanations or a sound-free mode. Free features compute on the device, so they keep running even as more people use them.

06

If it is not ready, we say so

We mark what you can use today separately from what we are still building, and we do not promise effects we have not measured.
