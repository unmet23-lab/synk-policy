Installing and opening the Mac preview

1. Open Apple menu → About This Mac. Choose Apple Silicon for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor. Both versions require macOS 13 or later.

2. Open the downloaded DMG, drag SYNKPlatformPreview into Applications, then launch it from Applications. Use the same steps when installing WORLD from the platform.

3. If macOS cannot verify the developer, confirm that you downloaded the app from SYNK, then use Open Anyway for that app in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Each app may need this confirmation the first time it opens.

Atlas

Eight engines that read one person

Eight engines share the work: understanding a person’s goal and situation, fitting the content, screens, words and sound to them, and checking whether it actually helped. We are building the role-play’s AI director on the same engines, to choose the next line and the next note.

  • UnderstandCoreDecides what is needed now
  • UnderstandStrataMaps what to learn, in what orderIn preparation
  • UnderstandTrailRecords the process and the change
  • ComposeLoomScreens that respond to touch
  • ComposeVellumExplanations people understand
  • ComposeReedMusic and sound for the scene
  • CheckPrismFinds patterns across many results
  • CheckTemperChecks whether it helped

Principles we keep

  • We do not guess from ageWe go by the settings a person chooses and what they actually did, not age, gender or nationality.
  • Your answers and AI guesses stay separateWhat you wrote and did is kept as the original; anything AI inferred is marked so you can correct it.
  • If it is not ready, we say soWe mark what you can use today separately from what we are still building, and we do not promise effects we have not checked.
I started SYNK because I wanted to give every student the attention they needed.

Yuho YangFounder, SYNK

I worked as a performer on stage and screen, then met my students as deputy director of an academy in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. I knew each one needed different help but never had the time to tailor it, so I built Atlas to do that with technology. Now I am putting what I learned in front of the camera into an AI director.
