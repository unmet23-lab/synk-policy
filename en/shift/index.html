Knowing an AI tool is different from using it at work. SHIFT is preparing training and consulting that help participants find practical uses for AI by creating and reviewing their own work.

Our process

Learn and create
through real work.

We use source materials, prompts, and revisions from building SYNK in our training. We adapt the approach to your task and provide both the finished work and guidance on using it.

01

Understand the task and goal

We identify who will use the work, what is difficult, and what result is needed, then agree on the scope.

02

Learn, practice, and create

We explain with examples relevant to your work, then help you try it yourself. We also cover how to assess and revise the results.

03

Review and hand over

We check the result in the environment where it will be used. Within the agreed scope, we hand over the finished work, source files, usage guidance, and a list of any further work needed.

The scope, schedule, and deliverables for training, consulting, and branding are agreed individually. The examples below are SYNK's own work.
