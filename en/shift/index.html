AI LECTURES · INSTRUCTORS · AUTOMATION

AI lectures, plus work automation

SYNK SHIFT teaches the skills the AI era calls for at schools, companies and institutions, then automates repetitive work with AI for companies that took a lecture, to cut fixed costs. In schools we also give AI career lectures on how to steer a career in the AI era and what to prepare for it. We also run an instructor agency that finds instructors and assigns them to fit each request.

01 / WHAT WE DO

Start with a lecture, then automate the work

We give AI lectures at schools, companies and institutions, and find and assign instructors who fit each request. When a company that took a lecture wants it, we pick one repetitive task and automate it.

01AI career lectures for schools

A good fit forschools that want their career education to reflect how AI is changing work

Not a general careers talk: a lecture on how to steer a career in the AI era and which skills to build for it.

For example

Second-year high school · careers class · everyone’s interests differ

  1. 01See how AI is changing work in each student’s field of interest
  2. 02Pick the skills that work needs and what to start on now
  3. 03Write a one-page career plan and talk it through
What you get
A one-page plan for each student — what AI is changing in their field, and what they will prepare
Agreed first
Year group and class size, lesson time, and the devices and tools students can use

02AI lectures for organisations

A good fit forcompanies, public bodies and schools that want staff to use AI in their actual work

Not a tour of tools: a hands-on lecture where each person makes one piece of work with AI from their own job.

For example

30 office staff · a team heavy on reports and email · mostly new to AI

  1. 01Pick practice tasks from the audience’s real work
  2. 02Each person makes one piece of work from their own material
  3. 03Practise checking and fixing what the AI produced
What you get
One piece of work per person, and a request–check–fix routine they can reuse
Agreed first
Learning goals and audience, the tools allowed and any extra cost, and material that must stay out of practice

03Instructor agency

A good fit forschools, companies and institutions that have a topic but struggle to find an AI or AI-career instructor who fits their audience

We hear the goal and the audience, then find and assign an instructor who fits. You know exactly which instructor is coming before you sign.

For example

High school careers week · the whole second year · a talk in the hall

  1. 01Pin down the goal and the audience
  2. 02Show the instructor’s evidence card and samples
  3. 03Sign once instructor, date and terms are confirmed
What you get
An instructor who fits your audience, with an evidence card — whose work they teach, what they have made themselves, samples they can share, and what stays after the class
Agreed first
Running the day, substitute instructors, cancellation, and questions after the lecture

04AI work automation

A good fit forsmall companies paying fixed costs for the same entry, sorting and reporting that people repeat every week

We pick one repetitive task and automate it with AI to help cut what it costs you. We also build in where a person checks, and what happens when it stops.

For example

Copying order emails into the ledger · every day · one person

  1. 01Map the current workflow together in a half-day review
  2. 02Demonstrate one automation on the spot and send a proposal
  3. 03Agree scope and cost, build it, and check it every month
What you get
One working automation, and a routine that alerts you and hands over to a person when it stops
Agreed first
What the task costs in time and money today, where a person checks, and who fixes it after handover

All four are in preparation, so we agree the audience and scope for each request. You can ask before everything is decided. We have not published a price list, and we do not promise learning results or savings in advance; we check them with you afterwards.

02 / HOW WE TEACH

Not a list of tools, but something to take away

An AI lecture is not a tour of tools. Each person makes one piece of work from their own career plans or job, and leaves with a way to do it again on their own.

  1. 01

    Start from the audience’s own work

    Students pick a field they care about; staff pick a task from their real job. We check the prior knowledge needed, the tools allowed and any extra cost in advance.

  2. 02

    Everyone leaves with one thing made

    Newcomers take away one finished piece of work; people who already use AI take away a request–check–fix routine to reuse.

  3. 03

    Checking and fixing, too

    We practise not taking an AI answer as it stands: where to check it and how to fix it. Material that must stay out of practice, and which questions we take, are agreed at the start.

We do not present one lecture’s results as every attendee’s skill gain or time saved. What stayed after the lecture is checked with whoever booked it.

03 / AUTOMATION

Automate one repetitive task, cut the fixed cost

If people repeat the same entry and sorting every week, we pick that one task and automate it with AI. The price is set against the cost it removes, measured before and after automation.

  1. 01

    Lecture

    First, look together at what your company can hand to AI and what should stay with people.

  2. 02

    Half-day review

    We map the workflow with you, demonstrate one automation on the spot, then send a proposal with scope and cost. You decide whether to build after reading it, and you can stop after the review.

  3. 03

    Build one task

    Scope and price are agreed up front, then we automate one task — including where a person checks and how it alerts you when it stops.

  4. 04

    Monthly care

    Each month we check it still runs and adjust it when the work or the services it uses change. Time and cost saved are measured before and after under the same conditions.

Along the way, we can also take on

Consulting

Work out which tasks to hand to AI first, and where a person should check.

Market research and product testing

Interview the people who will use it, watch them try it, and report where they get stuck.

Branding

Fit lecture material, notices and the screens automation adds to your company’s voice and look.

04 / PRICING

No price list yet, scope comes first

We have not set a price list for lectures or automation. We agree the audience and scope for each request before quoting, and tell you up front what the price depends on.

  1. 01

    Lectures

    Set by audience and size, length and number of sessions, the practice material to prepare and the venue. When we assign another instructor, their terms are confirmed too.

  2. 02

    Automation build

    Scope and price for one task are agreed in the half-day review. It depends on how many exceptions there are and which services it must connect to, and the price is based on the cost the automation removes.

  3. 03

    Monthly care

    We agree how often we check and what we fix, and take it on month by month. Fees for external services the automation uses may be extra, and if the work changes enough to need a rebuild, scope and cost are agreed again.

Savings are measured before and after under the same conditions, and we tell you what we compared against. We do not stretch one demonstration into a savings figure.
