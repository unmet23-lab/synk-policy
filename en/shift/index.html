SHIFT's vision & philosophy

Employees have different tasks, and customers have different needs. SHIFT starts with the problem to solve and is preparing training and consulting to help put AI to work. We are also developing personalized services that adjust the next step of support to each person’s goals and results.

Personalized services · In development

Each person’s needs and results
shape the next step of support

We are developing four services in two areas: coaching and training, and buying and product adoption support.

01

Personalized expert coaching

Adjust the next assignment and feedback to each person’s goals and submitted work.

02

Personalized buying and quote advice

Explain suitable configurations and why they fit each customer’s budget, use case, and existing equipment.

03

Product onboarding and adoption support

Guide customers from setup to completing real tasks, based on their role and progress.

Sales and customer service practice

All four services are in development. Available features and rollout dates will be announced separately.

Market research & product testing · In preparation

See how customers
actually use it

We are preparing a service to test how local users understand and use products, apps, and content before entering a new market.

01

Market response

Define the market and audience, then explore responses to products and messages through interviews and surveys.

02

Usability testing

Observe people using the product to find where they get stuck and what explanation they need.

03

Findings and next steps

Separate observations from interpretation and identify improvements to products, services, and content.

This service is in preparation. Markets, participants, schedules, and fees will be confirmed individually. Participants will give separate consent and receive compensation. Findings from a limited group will not be presented as representing an entire market.
