PERSONALIZED SERVICES FOR COMPANIES

Personal guidance for everyone, built from your material

SYNK SHIFT brings personalized services to work where every person needs different guidance — advice, onboarding, coaching and staff training. We connect your product information and rules to Atlas, and after launch we check with you that it is really used.

01 / SERVICES

Where we bring it in

Helping customers choose and use products, and tailoring expert coaching and staff training to each person. We start with one task at one company.

01Personalized purchase advice

A good fit forretailers and specialist shops with so many products and options that every enquiry starts again from budget and use

Customers enter their budget, what they need it for and what they already own, and first see a matching setup, the reasons for it, and the options they can skip.

For example

Laptop for video editing · fixed budget · often working on the road

  1. 01Choose the specs and weight the work needs
  2. 02Drop unnecessary options, with reasons
  3. 03Pass the summary to a salesperson for a quote
What we build
A web advice service that answers with your products, and a summary of each customer’s needs for your staff
What changes
Customers enquire after seeing products and reasons that fit them. Staff start with a quote, not a questionnaire.
Try the AI computer picks ↗

A public example built the same way, in Korean. Choose what you do and your budget, and it shows the specs that fit and why.

02Product onboarding

A good fit forsoftware and B2B companies whose new customers stall at setup or keep asking the same questions

We look at the customer’s role, goal and how far they have got, and show the next step one at a time.

For example

A sales team that just signed up · goal: manage customers

  1. 01Help them finish setup
  2. 02Get them to add their first customer
  3. 03Check the result and show the next step
What we build
A web guide customers follow on their own
What changes
Customers see their next step without waiting for support. Your support team spends time on problems that need a person.

03Expert coaching service

A good fit fortrainers, consultants and education companies who repeat the same tasks and explanations as clients grow

Your methods and standards become personal tasks and feedback for each client, and the next task changes with their results.

For example

Presentation coaching · one person over-explains, another struggles with questions

  1. 01Each person practises a different weakness
  2. 02The next task follows what they submit
  3. 03The coach reviews what needs a human eye
What we build
A client-facing coaching service with diagnosis, personal tasks and next steps
What changes
The service handles repeated guidance. Experts spend their time on judgement and deeper coaching.

04Sales and customer service training

A good fit forsales and CS teams and franchise head offices whose staff were trained but freeze in front of real customers

Staff rehearse realistic situations using your products and service standards. What they miss comes back in the next round.

For example

New employee · a customer says the price is too high

  1. 01Answer the customer directly
  2. 02See which standard you left out
  3. 03Practise the same situation again
What we build
A staff training tool with role-play and follow-up tasks
What changes
Staff handle hard conversations several times before the real thing. Managers see what each person misses.

These four are candidate services, in preparation. Features, who runs them, schedule and price are agreed with each company, and results for your customers are checked together after launch.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Ask, recommend, then adjust to the result

We do not build a new engine for each company. We add modules for product information and expert standards to Atlas, and SHIFT connects your material, rules and systems.

  1. 01

    Ask only what matters

    Start with the conditions that change the choice, and never ask the same thing twice.

  2. 02

    Recommend with reasons

    Show why something is recommended or left out, so customers can judge the fit themselves.

  3. 03

    Adjust to the result

    See where people got stuck and change the next guidance or practice — everyone falls short in different places.

User conditions and records are used only for the purpose and consent agreed when the service is set up.

03 / PROCESS

Pick one task, and see it through to operation

We start by agreeing who needs to finish what. After testing in the real environment, we also agree who runs the service and who handles exceptions.

  1. 01

    Task and definition of done

    Agree who finishes what and how it is done today, plus the material to use, the scope of personal data, and exceptions a person must check.

  2. 02

    User research and testing

    Interview the people who will use it and watch where they stop when they try it.

  3. 03

    Connection and staff readiness

    Connect your material and systems, and train staff to check results and handle exceptions.

  4. 04

    Results and operations

    Review completions, errors, handling time and support load together, and decide the next improvement.

Along the way, we can also take on

Staff training and consulting

Staff learn to use the service on real work material, check results and handle exceptions.

Automation and system connections

Split the steps the service handles from those a person checks, and connect repetitive work to your existing systems.

Service copy and screen branding

Make it obvious to customers who gets what help, with copy, screens and guides that fit your brand.

04 / PRICING

Build and run, priced separately

Building the service and using and running it afterwards are contracted separately. We also agree in advance what happens when usage grows or new features are needed.

  1. 01

    Initial setup

    Organise the task and material, configure the service and connect it to your systems, with agreed deliverables and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02

    Subscription and operation

    Service use, quality checks and agreed operational support, with usage, content updates and error response defined.

  3. 03

    Extra usage and development

    Usage beyond the plan, new connections or feature changes go ahead only after scope and cost are confirmed.

Prices and contract terms are set per project. Once we have confirmed that the same service can be supplied to several companies, we plan to look at an API and engine licensing.
