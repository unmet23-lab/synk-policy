Our process

through real work

Bringing a soft texture to the screen

We wanted to make the characters and objects from our screens in the real world, too. We designed their soft textures with objects you could touch and keep nearby in mind.

We built the Loom engine to bring those textures to the screen and make them feel alive through small movements and responses.
