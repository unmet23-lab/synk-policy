SYNK SHIFTAI lectures and work automation

AI lectures, plus work automation

SYNK SHIFT teaches the skills the AI era calls for at schools, companies and institutions, then automates repetitive work with AI for companies that took a lecture, to cut fixed costs. In schools we also give AI career lectures on how to steer a career in the AI era and what to prepare for it. We also run an instructor agency that finds instructors and assigns them to fit each request.

Services

Start with a lecture, then automate the work

We give AI lectures at schools, companies and institutions, and find and assign instructors who fit each request. When a company that took a lecture wants it, we pick one repetitive task and automate it.

  1. 01

    Hands-on AI lecture

    Not a tour of tools: a hands-on lecture where each person makes one piece of work with AI from their own job.

    A good fit for
    companies, public bodies and schools that want staff to use AI in their actual work
    What you get
    One piece of work per person, and a request–check–fix routine they can reuse
    Agreed first
    Learning goals and audience, the tools allowed and any extra cost, and material that must not be used in the exercises
    How it runs
    1. Pick practice tasks from the audience’s real work
    2. Each person makes one piece of work from their own material
    3. Practise checking and fixing what the AI produced

    For example “30 office staff · a team heavy on reports and email · mostly new to AI”

  2. 02

    AI career lectures for schools

    Not a general careers talk: a lecture on how to steer a career in the AI era and which skills to build for it.

    A good fit for
    schools that want their career education to reflect how AI is changing work
    What you get
    A one-page plan for each student — what AI is changing in their field, and what they will prepare
    Agreed first
    Year group and class size, lesson time, and the devices and tools students can use
    How it runs
    1. See how AI is changing work in each student’s field of interest
    2. Pick the skills that kind of work needs, and what to start on now
    3. Write a one-page career plan and talk it through

    For example “Second-year high school · careers class · everyone’s interests differ”

  3. 03

    Instructor agency

    We hear the goal and the audience, then find and assign an instructor who fits. You know exactly which instructor is coming before you sign.

    A good fit for
    schools, companies and institutions that have a topic but struggle to find an AI or AI-career instructor who fits their audience
    What you get
    An instructor who fits your audience, with an evidence card — who they teach and for what kind of work, what they have made themselves, samples they can share, and what stays after the class
    Agreed first
    Running the day, substitute instructors, cancellation, and questions after the lecture
    How it runs
    1. Pin down the goal and the audience
    2. Show the instructor’s evidence card and samples
    3. Sign once instructor, date and terms are confirmed

    For example “High school careers week · the whole second year · a talk in the hall”

  4. 04

    AI work automation

    We pick one repetitive task and automate it with AI to help cut what it costs you. We also build in where a person checks, and what happens when it stops.

    A good fit for
    small companies paying fixed costs for the same entry, sorting and reporting that people repeat every week
    What you get
    One working automation, and a routine that alerts you and hands over to a person when it stops
    Agreed first
    What the task costs in time and money today, where a person checks, and who fixes it after handover
    How it runs
    1. Map the current workflow together in a half-day review
    2. Demonstrate one automation on the spot and send a proposal
    3. Agree scope and cost, build it, and check it every month

    For example “Copying order emails into the ledger · every day · one person”

Not a list of tools, but something to take away

An AI lecture is not a tour of tools. Each person makes one piece of work from their own career plans or job, and leaves with a way to do it again on their own.

  1. Start from the audience’s own work — Students pick a field they care about; staff pick a task from their real job. We check the prior knowledge needed, the tools allowed and any extra cost in advance.
  2. Everyone leaves with one thing made — Newcomers take away one finished piece of work; people who already use AI take away a request–check–fix routine to reuse.
  3. Checking and fixing, too — Instead of taking an AI answer at face value, we practise where to check it and how to fix it. Material that must not be used in the exercises, and which questions we take, are agreed at the start.

AutomationTaking enquiries · Quoted per request

Automate one repetitive task, cut the fixed cost

If people repeat the same entry and sorting every week, we pick that one task and automate it with AI. The price is based on the cost it saves, before versus after automation.

  1. 01Lecture

    First, look together at what your company can hand to AI and what should stay with people.

  2. 02Half-day review

    We map the workflow with you, demonstrate one automation on the spot, then send a proposal with scope and cost. You decide whether to build after reading it, and you can stop after the review.

  3. 03Build one task

    Scope and price are agreed up front, then we automate one task — including where a person checks and how it alerts you when it stops.

  4. 04Monthly care

    Each month we check it still runs and adjust it when the work or the services it uses change. Time and cost saved are measured before and after under the same conditions, and we report them to you.

Pricing

Scope first, then a quote

We agree the audience and scope for each request before quoting, and tell you up front what the price depends on.

  1. 01

    Lectures

    Set by audience and size, length and number of sessions, the practice material to prepare and the venue. When we assign another instructor, their terms are confirmed too.

  2. 02

    Automation build

    Scope and price for one task are agreed in the half-day review. It depends on how many exceptions there are and which services it must connect to, and the price is based on the cost the automation saves.

  3. 03

    Monthly care

    We agree how often we check and what we fix, and take it on month by month. Fees for external services the automation uses may be extra, and if the work changes enough to need a rebuild, scope and cost are agreed again.

Savings are measured before and after under the same conditions, and we tell you what we compared against.
