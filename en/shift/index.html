Personalization use cases · In preparation

Choose one task
to introduce first

We are preparing personalized services in two areas: purchasing and product use, and coaching and training. They help customers choose and use products, or adapt expert coaching and staff training to each person. We agree together on the task and features to introduce.

01

Personalized buying and quote advice

Sellers and specialist shops with many products and options, where every consultation starts by asking about budget and use again

When a customer enters their budget, intended use, and the equipment they already have, it first shows a fitting set, the reasons for it, and the options they can leave out.

Example

“Laptop for video editing · fixed budget · often out of the office”

  1. Picks the specs and weight the work needs
  2. Leaves out unneeded options, with the reason
  3. Passes the organized requirements to your staff for a quote
What you get
A web consultation service that answers with your products, and a summary of each customer’s requirements for your staff.
What changes
Customers enquire after seeing the products that fit them and why. Your staff receive organized requirements and start with the quote.
02

Product onboarding and adoption support

Software and B2B service companies whose new customers stall at setup or keep asking the same usage questions

It looks at the customer’s role, goal, and how far they have got, and guides them through what to do now, one step at a time.

Example

“A sales team new to the product · goal: managing customers”

  1. Helps them finish the initial setup
  2. Has them register a first customer
  3. Checks what they did and guides the next step
What you get
A web guide your customers follow by themselves.
What changes
Customers see the next step right away instead of waiting for a reply. Your support team spends its time on problems that need a person.
03

Expert coaching service development

Instructors, consultants, and training companies who repeat the same tasks and explanations one by one as clients grow

It carries the expert’s method and criteria, giving each client different tasks and feedback. It changes the next task after seeing the results.

Example

“Presentation coaching · one client runs long, another struggles to answer questions”

  1. The two practise different parts
  2. The next task follows what they submit
  3. The coach checks what needs a person
What you get
A coaching service for clients, with diagnosis, individual tasks, and follow-up guidance in one flow.
What changes
The service takes over the repeated guidance. The expert spends time on the judgement and advanced coaching that need them in person.
04

Sales and customer service practice

Sales and customer-service teams and franchise head offices whose staff were trained but cannot put it into words in front of a real customer

Staff practise realistic situations with your products and service standards. What they miss comes back in the next practice.

Example

“A new employee · a customer who says the price is too high”

  1. They answer the customer themselves
  2. It checks which standards the answer left out
  3. They practise the same situation again
What you get
A training tool for staff, with situation practice and follow-up tasks.
What changes
Staff try difficult replies many times before the real thing. Managers see what each person missed and coach from there.

What Atlas does

It asks, recommends,
and adjusts after seeing the result

Atlas is the name of eight shared engines developed by SYNK. We combine them with modules for product information and specialist criteria, rather than rebuilding the shared engines for each company. SHIFT connects client materials, rules and systems, and defines completion criteria and operating methods for the chosen task.

01

It asks only what it needs

It starts with the requirements that change the choice, and is built not to ask again what was already answered.

02

It recommends with reasons

It shows why something is recommended or left out, so customers can judge whether it fits them.

03

It adjusts after seeing the result

It sees where someone got stuck and changes the next guidance and practice, because each person falls short in different places.

User conditions and activity records are used within the purposes and consent agreed for the actual introduction. Local engine validation is not presented as evidence of client results.

Market research and product testing
for the service you plan to introduce

We research the customers and environments for a personalized service, then use hands-on product tests to identify improvements. We check who needs what and whether the service helps more than the existing approach.

01

User and market research

We define the users and task, and examine their conditions and difficulties with the existing method through interviews and materials.

02

Product usability testing

We observe where participants stop and which explanations they need while using the service.

03

Check results and operating work

Our process

Check operation after introduction

We begin with the users and the result they should complete. We connect company materials and rules, check the service in its actual environment, and agree who will operate it and handle exceptions.

Introduction and service agreements

From the initial setup
to ongoing use and operation

Initial setup and ongoing use and operation are scoped separately in the agreement. We also agree in advance on the scope and cost of additional usage or new features.

01

Initial setup fee

We organize the task and materials, configure the service and connect it with existing systems. Deliverables and acceptance criteria are agreed together.

02

Recurring service and operation fee

This covers service use, quality checks and agreed operational support. We define usage allowances, material updates, support hours and error handling.

03

Additional usage and development fees

We confirm the scope and cost in advance for usage beyond the allowance, new system connections or feature changes. Agreed error fixes remain within the existing responsibilities.

Pricing, allowances and contract terms have not yet been set and will be discussed for each task and operating scope.
