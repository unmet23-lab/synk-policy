01 / STUDY & WORK

In preparation

A study plan built on your major and career

Compare schools by the conditions that matter for your major and career, and set a preparation timeline that fits your Korean level and the time you have.

See LAB’s Korean course ↗
  1. 01

    Study plan

    Organise your goals, budget, Korean level and application timing, compare schools and set a timeline.

  2. 02

    Application support

    Polish the documents you wrote and practise interviews, and check deadlines, paperwork and what to do before you leave.

  3. 03

    Korean for real situations

    From asking questions in class to answering interview questions, use the Korean you learned at LAB as if it were real.

The scope, schedule and cost of support are agreed after a consultation.

02 / VR PRACTICE

In preparation

Practise first meetings in VR before they happen

Explain your study plan in a visa interview, ask a question in a university class, and talk about your experience in a job interview. Answering out loud shows which expressions are hard and what you still need to prepare.

Visa interview

Sample question “What would you like to study in Korea?”

Explain in your own words why you want to study and what your plan is.

University life

Sample question “How do I say which part of the group project I will take?”

Practise asking in class and dividing roles with teammates.

Job interviews

Sample question “What problem did you solve in that project?”

Connect your role and what you solved, and get ready for follow-up questions.

We are preparing this as VR lessons alongside LAB’s Korean classes. VR practice is not work experience or a qualification and does not guarantee admission, a visa or a job.

03 / PORTFOLIO

Show what you learned and what you have done

Why you chose your field and what you have done so far, shown through your study plan and work you made yourself.

  1. 01

    For students

    Explain your experience with concrete examples, and find what to strengthen.

  2. 02

    For schools and companies

    See motivation, learning and roles through the material the student chose to share.

Portfolios share only material the student has reviewed and agreed to share. Learning records are never sent automatically.

04 / SETTLE IN

In preparation

Finding a place to live in Korea

We are preparing support that connects people coming to Korea for study or work with housing operators, so they can choose where to live and start daily life.

  1. 01

    Conditions and comparison

    Set out your length of stay, budget and distance to school, and compare the homes available.

  2. 02

    Move-in and daily life

    Move-in date, what to bring, how the facilities work — what you need at the start.

  3. 03

    When something goes wrong

    We help you report facility or living problems to the operator.

Partner operators, areas and costs are not decided yet, and we are not taking move-in bookings. Contracts and building management are handled by the operator.
