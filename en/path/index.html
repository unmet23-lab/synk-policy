The road

From packing your bags to settling in

Two things are open now: interview practice and a day in Seoul. The other four open in the order you meet them.

  1. A day in Seoul

    Pick what you feel like and how long you have, and your day is planned — no sign-up.

    Try it above ↑

  2. Preparing to study

    Preparing to study in Korea around your major and career.

    What it covers

    We are getting ready to compare schools with you by the conditions that matter for your major and career, and to set a timeline that fits your Korean level and the time you have.

    Study plan Organize your goals, budget, Korean level and application timing, compare candidate schools and set a timeline.

    Applications and interviews Polish the application documents you have written, practise for interviews, and check deadlines and what to do before you leave.

    Portfolio Show what you have learned and done through your study plan and your own work. Only material you have checked and agreed to share is ever shared.

    The scope, schedule and cost of support are agreed after a consultation.

    LAB’s Korean course →

  3. Job requirements

    For the job you want, the requirements first, in order.

    What it covers

    Working in Korea takes a different visa, qualifications and level of Korean in each field. For fields that need a qualification, such as care work or manufacturing, we are preparing to set out what you need and by when, step by step and from your point of view.

    • Visa, qualification and Korean requirements by field
    • Qualification training and exams follow the accredited bodies’ guidance
    • Reminders for exam and document deadlines, and interview practice

  4. Interview practice

    Rehearse a caregiver interview in 60–90 seconds.

    Listen above ↑

  5. Housing

    Finding a place to live in Korea, with you.

    What it covers

    We are preparing to help people coming to Korea for study or work choose where to live and start daily life.

    Conditions and comparison Set out your length of stay, budget and distance to school or work, and compare the homes you can choose from.

    Moving in Find out what you need at the start, such as move-in dates, what to bring and how the facilities work.

    When something goes wrong Get help reporting problems with the building or daily life to the operator.

    Partner operators, areas and costs are not decided yet, and we are not taking move-in bookings. Contracts and the running of buildings are handled by the operator.

  6. Settle Together

    Your first 30 days of to-dos, seen through with you.

    2027 pilot

    What it covers

    Settle Together stays beside people who have just arrived in Korea, from the first day until they have settled in. It does not stop at telling you what must be done in the first 30 days — registering as a foreign resident, opening a bank account, health insurance — but checks with each person that it is done.

    Quarterly settlement indicators Every quarter we measure the share who finished the required paperwork of the first 30 days, how long it took to first answer a question, the share of people who lost touch and were found and helped, the share who came back to meetups, and how many Koreans took part. Partner institutions receive only totals in which no one can be identified.

    Principles we keep You take part only with your consent, and we do not take foreign resident registration numbers or passport numbers. What you tell us is not used for enforcement, and participants pay nothing for the core support.

    The 2027 pilot From March to August 2027 (spring semester and summer break), we are preparing a pilot in which a dedicated manager and senior international students look after 30–50 people. App notifications and the AI first reply will be open to up to 200 people once the app is released (target: February).

    The app screens are mockups in Korean, and the names, numbers and dates in them are examples. Existing institutions keep their counselling and education as they are; we add follow-through to the end and settlement indicators. We are not taking applications yet.
