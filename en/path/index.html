Study & career support for international students · In preparation

Prepare for study and work in Korea, together.

SYNK PATH is a study, daily life, and career support brand for international students preparing to study in Korea. We plan with you, from choosing a school, learning Korean, and preparing for interviews to considering your career after graduation. We are preparing a service that combines LAB's Korean lessons with VR practice for situations in Korea and helps you organize your preparation into a portfolio.

Life & careers in Korea

Build independence in
studying and living in Korea.

Korean can be useful before you arrive in Korea, starting with visa preparation. If an embassy or consulate requests an interview during the visa review, you need to be ready to explain your reasons for studying and your study plans in your own words. After arrival, you can use your Korean to explain the purpose of your visit at immigration, ask professors questions, and describe your experience in job interviews. PATH is preparing support that combines LAB's Korean lessons, everyday and interview practice, and career guidance.

Study planning & learning

Plan your studies around
your major and career goals.

We compare schools' application requirements based on your intended major and career after graduation, then map out your preparation schedule. Taking your current Korean level and available study time into account, we guide you step by step through language learning, application documents, and interview practice.

01

Practice Korean for real situations

Practice everything from classroom questions to interview answers.＋

When asking a professor a question or explaining your motivation in an interview, you need to put your Korean into use. The approach is to try expressions learned at LAB in VR situations, then practice difficult expressions again in class.

02

Update the plan as your goals change

Adjust your plan to your interests, career goals, and learning progress.＋

Exploring a major or role may spark an interest in another field, or learning Korean may take longer than expected. We review what you want to change and your current progress, then adjust your school choices, learning plan, and preparation schedule.

Portfolios & connections with schools and employers

Show what you have learned
and done in a portfolio.

When applying to a school or employer, you need to explain why you chose your field and what you have done to prepare. PATH helps organize study plans, your own writing and presentations, and project work into a portfolio that shows your experience and strengths.

01

Help students explain their experience

Include study plans and work you have created yourself in your portfolio.＋

Use your work to explain what you were aiming to learn, your role in a project, and how you handled challenges. This gives you specific examples for applications and interviews and helps you identify what to improve next.

02

Help schools and employers understand applicants

Consider motivation, learning progress, and actual work together.＋

Materials selected by the student can show their motivation, learning process, and role in projects. PATH is preparing connections with schools and employers by reviewing students' career goals and institutions' admission or recruitment requirements. Institutions can use this fuller picture to make selection and support decisions.

We share only materials the student has reviewed and agreed to share. Learning records are not sent automatically, and VR practice results are not presented as work experience or accredited qualifications.
