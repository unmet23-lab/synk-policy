Seoul travel · free experience

The same city,
a trip that feels like you

Build a day around real places in Jongno, Bukchon and Seochon. Set your interests, budget, companions and walking needs, then compare plans of two to five stops with photos, a map and a timetable.

01

A day that fits your needs

Set your start and finish times, starting point, budget and walking limits. See confirmed costs and details that need checking, then keep or replace the places you choose.

02

Include your companions

Enter each companion’s likes, things to avoid and requirements. See how each person’s preferences are reflected and choose a plan together.

03

Adjust as the day unfolds

Mark places you have visited or want to skip, then compare plans for your remaining time and budget. Review the new itinerary before applying changes such as less walking or more time indoors.

Seoul itineraries are free and can be saved with SYNK ID. Recheck opening hours and costs before visiting. Travel across other countries, accommodation and booking services are in preparation.

Housing & settling in · In preparation

Finding a place to live in Korea,
together

We are preparing support that connects people coming to Korea to study or work with housing operators, so they can choose where to live and start daily life after moving in.

01

Sort & compare requirements

We sort out the length of stay, budget, and distance to school, and compare the homes you can choose from.

02

Move in & get settled

We tell you what you need at the start, such as the move-in date, what to bring, and how to use the facilities.

03

When a problem comes up

If a problem comes up with the facilities or daily life, we help you tell the operator.

Partner operators, areas, and costs are not decided yet, and we are not taking move-in bookings. The operator handles the actual contract and facility management; we check its terms and explain them.
